Frequently asked

How the Glow Blog works, and how to get an answer we haven't written yet.

Are these articles reviewed by a professional?

Yes. Every piece is written by our formulation team and read by a consultant dermatologist before it goes live. Where a claim comes from a specific study, we say so in the article rather than rounding it up to "clinically proven".

Will you tell me to buy something in every post?

No. Plenty of our advice costs nothing — using lukewarm water, applying serum to damp skin, giving an active six weeks before judging it.

When we do mention a Lumiere product it's because it fits the point being made, and we'll happily name the ingredient so you can find it elsewhere.

Can I ask a question or suggest a topic?

Please do. Send it through the contact form and an advisor will reply directly — usually within two working days. The questions we get asked most often become the next articles.
