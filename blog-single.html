The Ultimate Evening Routine for Overnight Repair

Nighttime is when your skin goes into repair mode. The products you apply before bed have the crucial job of supporting this natural regeneration process. But are you applying them in the right order?

1. The Double Cleanse

The foundation of any good evening routine is a thoroughly cleansed face. Start with an oil-based cleanser to melt away sunscreen, makeup, and excess sebum. Follow up with a gentle water-based cleanser, like our Oat Milk Cleanser, to wash away any remaining impurities without stripping your skin's natural moisture barrier.

"A clean canvas ensures that your expensive serums can actually penetrate the skin rather than sitting on top of a layer of daytime grime."

2. Hydration is Key

Once your face is clean and slightly damp, it's time for hydration. Apply a humectant-rich serum. Our Dewy Hydrating Serum, packed with 2% Hyaluronic Acid and Panthenol, is designed specifically for this step. It acts like a sponge, pulling water into the deeper layers of your skin.

3. Seal it in

The final step is crucial: locking in that hydration. A good moisturizer acts as a seal. If you have a compromised skin barrier, opt for something rich in Ceramides, like our Barrier Repair Cream. It mimics your skin's natural lipids to restore balance and prevent transepidermal water loss while you sleep.
